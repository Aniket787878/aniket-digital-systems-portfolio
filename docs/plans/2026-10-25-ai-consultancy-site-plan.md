# AI consultancy site: plan, positioning, competitors, name (25 Oct 2026)

Status: PLAN ONLY. Nothing here is built. Builds start only when Aniket says go.
Builds on the 9 Oct funnel audit (`docs/outreach/2026-10-09-ai-consultancy-funnel-audit.md`, 14 competitors, uncommitted) and the ICP decision in `docs/system/05-icp-positioning.md`.

## 1. The decision in one line

Launch a separate, single-purpose AI consultancy brand and site, fed by the portfolio, with one promise: **the repeated front-desk work of a service business (enquiries, bookings, follow-ups, reminders, notes) handled by AI, checked by a person, live in weeks.**

The portfolio stays as Aniket's personal site (websites + software + AI). The new site is the company. The clinic platform, the Admin Desk demo and the three working demo tools are the proof, not the market.

## 2. Positioning

### Who it is for
Owner-run service businesses in India first, English-speaking markets second: clinics and wellness practices, studios (fitness, dance, music), coaching and tuition, agencies and consultancies, real estate and small professional firms. 2 to 30 staff. They lose money between "someone asked" and "someone answered".

### The one problem we own
**Slow or missed replies.** Every competitor sells "AI automation" in general. We sell one outcome: nothing a customer sends goes unanswered, unbooked or unfollowed. Everything else (notes, invoices, reports) is "and then".

### The promise (hero candidates, pick one)
1. "Every enquiry answered. Every booking confirmed. Every follow-up sent. By AI, checked by a person."
2. "Hand the repeated work to AI. Keep the decisions." (already live on the portfolio; reuse or retire)
3. "Your front desk, running itself."

### Why us (the three things competitors cannot say)
1. **Built into working software, not just chat.** The AI sits inside a booking system that runs a real practice every day, not a demo chatbot.
2. **Try it before you talk to anyone.** Live demo tools on a dummy clinic (Appointment Desk, Knowledge Assistant, Website Widget).
3. **Fixed date, in writing, or the second half is free.** The existing guarantee, lifted to the hero.

### What we are not
Not an LLM lab (that is what Sarvam AI is). Not a "₹15k full automation" shop. Not a general AI agency doing everything for everyone. Not clinic-only.

### Voice
Plain words, no dev jargon on any visible page (same rule as the portfolio). Prices shown. Reply time shown. Guarantee quoted.

## 3. Competitors (what we are up against)

### From the 9 Oct audit (14 sites, India + international)
- 13 of 14 offer a free diagnostic (AI check, audit, snapshot) before any sales talk.
- Most have one "book a call" button to a calendar; India sites add WhatsApp and a 24-48h reply promise.
- Proof is one hard number per case ("12 hours a week saved"); almost nobody has a live demo you can try.
- Paid diagnostic credited to the build (Free Me Up, Mayank Digital Labs) is the best ladder and matches our Roadmap.
- Weak spots across the board: hidden prices, generic 4-step process diagrams, stat-only proof, vague guarantees.

What the fresh read adds to the 9 Oct audit:
- Entry point is almost always a free 30-minute call; only Teknesya and Free Me Up give something before the call.
- Only Mayank and Free Me Up publish prices. Everyone else hides them.
- Proof is logos, founder-estimated totals and "happy client" counts. Almost no named before/after.
- Most sites list 10 to 25 services and read as generalist shops. Industry lists exist; vertical depth does not.
- Copy is tech-first ("agents", "pipelines", "RAG"). Owner outcomes are rare.
- Nobody except Free Me Up mentions data handling or privacy. For Indian clinics and consultancies under the DPDP Act this is an open door.
- Nobody sells handover: staff training and a written playbook the client keeps.

### Price bands (India, published Oct 2026)
- Single workflow: ₹20k to ₹45k. Starter packages: ₹50k to ₹75k. Multi-system: ₹1.2L to ₹3L.
- Retainers: ₹15k to ₹75k a month. Model/WhatsApp/hosting costs billed on top.
- International paid diagnostics about $500; pilots $4.5k to $5.5k; subscriptions £350 to £950 a month.
- Our live prices (Roadmap ₹20k/$490 credited, Quick Win ₹30k/$690, sprints and assistants above) sit in the premium-but-normal band. Keep them. Add running costs (model, WhatsApp, hosting) as a stated pass-through.

### Gaps we can own
1. A free check that gives an instant, honest on-screen answer (not "we'll email you").
2. A live demo the visitor can use in 30 seconds.
3. A guarantee people can quote.
4. One vertical page per buyer type with a worked example (clinic, studio, agency, coaching), not one generic page.
5. Transparent "what AI costs to run each month" line. Nobody shows it.
6. A plain data-handling promise (where client data lives, who can see it, DPDP Act wording). Only one site in 25 does this.
7. Handover as part of the price: staff trained, a written playbook they keep, no lock-in.

## 4. Name

### Sarvam AI: cannot be used
Sarvam AI is a Bengaluru LLM company founded 2023 (Vivek Raghavan, Pratyush Kumar), raised $41M in Dec 2023, in talks for a much larger round in 2026, launched 30B and 105B models at the India AI Impact Summit in Feb 2026. It is the best-known AI brand in India. sarvam.ai and sarvamai.com are taken. Using the name invites a trademark dispute and makes us look like a copy.

### What the name must do
Short (2 or 3 syllables), says "it is handled / it runs", easy to say in English and Hindi, free .ai or .com or .co, no serious conflict in AI or software, works as "<Name> handles it" in a sentence.

### Checked and ruled out (serious conflicts or domains gone)
Sahaj (Sahaj Software, 150+ staff), Yukti (Pulp Strategy bot), Pravah (Stanford-founded grid AI), Gati (Gatik AI, Allcargo Gati), Dhruv (Thomas Cook AI assistant), Sahayak (Evynta product, Sahay AI), Sutra (three AI products), Samarth, Saral, Anvay, Uttar (clear names but every .ai and .com gone), Sulabh (domains free but the word belongs to Sulabh International toilets in every Indian mind).

## 5. The site (what to build, in order)

### Stack
Same as the portfolio: React + Vite on Vercel, Lenis scroll, the existing design system, the existing n8n lead intake (v2, dUHbHJPBhHFB79qu) with a `source=<newsite>` field. No new backend. Cal.com link required before launch (still on him).

### Pages (launch set)
1. **Home**: hero promise + one button ("Get your free AI check"), reply-time and guarantee line, the cost-of-slow-replies calculator (3 inputs), "what AI takes off your plate" (4 jobs, each with a try-it demo), proof (clinic system with one hard number + Admin Desk demo), how it works (Free check > Roadmap, credited > Build by a date > Care), prices, questions to ask any AI vendor, FAQ, final CTA.
2. **Free AI check** (/check): 6 to 8 plain questions, instant on-screen result (top 3 jobs to hand to AI + rough hours saved, labelled an estimate), answers posted to n8n.
3. **Try it** (/try): the three demo tools on the dummy clinic, each with a one-line "what you are seeing".
4. **For clinics / studios / agencies / coaching** (/for/<type>): one worked example each, same template, honest demo labels.
5. **Pricing** (/pricing): Roadmap, Quick Win, Sprint, Assistant, Care; running costs stated; guarantee; 50/50 terms.
6. **Proof** (/work): the anonymised real projects + labelled demos (reuse portfolio cards and covers).
7. **About**: Aniket + how the clinic system came to be. No "solo" framing.
8. **Book** (/book): Cal.com embed, WhatsApp as the one backup.

### Later (after 2 to 3 paid engagements)
Testimonials, case studies with numbers, blog for the "AI for <vertical>" searches, Hindi copy toggle.

### Build flow (house rule)
`build` skill: spec + slices approved by him > Sonnet builder per slice in a worktree > gate (lint, typecheck, tests, Playwright click-through, screenshots) > one review > merge on "merge". Design through the `design` skill: audit, one named direction, proof on `review-shots`.

Suggested slices (each one PR):
1. Repo + brand tokens + home hero + nav + footer + n8n hookup (source tag).
2. Free AI check page with instant result.
3. Try-it page reusing the demo tools.
4. Vertical pages (4) from one template.
5. Pricing + proof + about + book.
6. SEO, OG image, analytics, accessibility audit, device matrix, launch.

Rough effort: 6 PRs, about 2 to 3 weeks of sessions at the usual pace, cloud sessions for builds, server only for the n8n source tag.
