# Fee List

> Demo Physio Clinic is a fictional clinic. All fees below are invented sample data for a portfolio demo. Prices are in Indian rupees (₹).

Per-session prices for each service and duration are in the Services Table. This document covers everything else that can appear on a bill.

## Payment
We accept card, bank transfer and cash at the front desk. Payment is due at the end of each session. A receipt is issued for every payment and can be used for insurance claims.

## Session packs
A pack of 5 follow-up sessions of the same length is charged at 10% less than five single sessions. Packs are valid for 4 months from the first session and cannot be shared between patients. Unused sessions are not refundable after the 4 months.

## Other charges
- Late cancellation fee: 50% of the session price (see the Cancellation Policy).
- No-show fee: 100% of the session price.
- Written report or fit-note letter: ₹300, ready within 3 working days.
- Copy of your records: free for the first request, ₹150 for each further request in the same year.
- Extra receipt copies: free.

## Fee changes
Fees are reviewed once a year in January. Patients are told about changes at least 30 days in advance.
