## North star

Turn the site from a portfolio into a **conversion machine** — a place where a warm cold lead lands, understands what you sell in <30s, sees proof, and books a call.

Everything in this repo either serves that north star or gets cut.

## Operating principles (from /autoplan)

1. **Completeness** — ship whole flows, not half-features
2. **Boil lakes** — fix everything in the immediate blast radius
3. **Pragmatic** — 5s decisions, not 5min debates
4. **DRY** — reuse before rebuild
5. **Explicit over clever** — a new reader gets it in 30s
6. **Bias to action** — ship, then iterate
