## 1. Pick a lane for outreach (the website can stay broad)

Outreach only works when the message is specific. Your proof is strongest for
**appointment-based businesses that live on WhatsApp**:

- Primary: therapy / psychology / wellness clinics, physiotherapy, dental, dermatology
- Secondary: salons, fitness / yoga studios, coaching institutes, diagnostic labs

Why: every one of them has the same pain your live system already solves —
bookings over WhatsApp, no-shows, forms on paper, admin at night.

The site can keep the broad "complete systems" headline. **Your cold messages,
ads and content should speak only to one lane for the first 90 days.**
Broad messaging from someone with zero reviews gets ignored.

## 2. The offer ladder

Small first step, bigger follow-on. First-3-client pricing is lower on purpose —
you are buying a testimonial, a case study and a referral.

| Offer | What they get | First-3 price | Normal price | Live in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **No-Show Fix** (entry) | WhatsApp confirmation + 24h/1h reminders + a reschedule link, on their existing calendar | ₹15k–25k | ₹30k–40k | 7 days |
| **Clinic Autopilot** (core) | Online booking + reminders + intake/consent form → PDF on WhatsApp + daily digest to the owner | ₹40k–60k | ₹60k–1L | 14 days |
| **WhatsApp / Website AI Assistant** | Answers FAQs, pricing, timings, collects leads, books slots, hands off to a human | ₹25k–50k | ₹50k–80k | 10 days |
| **Care Plan** (retainer) | Monitoring, fixes within 24h, small monthly changes | ₹5k–10k/mo | ₹15k–30k/mo | — |

Rules:
- Fixed price, written one-page scope, 50% up front.
- Discount only in exchange for: a named testimonial, permission to publish an anonymised case study, and two introductions.
- Running costs (WhatsApp API messages, hosting) are billed to *their* accounts — say so in the proposal.

## 3. Proof assets — build these in week 1–2, before any outreach

Nothing here requires a client. All of it must use **fake data** — the clinic
holds mental-health records.

1. **Demo clinic ("Sunrise Physio" or similar)** — a copy of the booking +
   reminder flow running on dummy therapists and a test WhatsApp number.
   Anyone can book a fake slot and *receive the WhatsApp message on their own
   phone*. This is your single best sales tool.
2. **Demo AI assistant** — a WhatsApp or website chatbot for the demo clinic
   that answers questions and books into the same demo calendar. Put a
   "Try it" button on the portfolio.
3. **90-second Loom** — client books → WhatsApp confirmation arrives → reminder
   arrives → desk screen shows it. No voice-over jargon; talk like an owner.
4. **Case study page** — "How an 11-therapist clinic runs bookings, reminders
   and paperwork on autopilot." Before → what was built → after → quote.
5. **Testimonial from the clinic** — ask the clinic director / a therapist for
   3 sentences + name + role. You work with them; this is the warmest ask you
   will ever make. Do it this week.
6. **5 short screen recordings (15–30s each)** — a WhatsApp reminder arriving,
   the n8n canvas running, the AI note appearing. These become reels and ad
   creatives.
7. **Portfolio fixes** (from the audit): outcome headline, restore the proof
   line, real screenshots, remove empty placeholders, set
   `VITE_LEAD_WEBHOOK_URL`, add a booking link for the free audit.

## 4. Channels — in the order to switch them on

### A. Warm network (week 2) — highest conversion, zero cost
- List 50 people: friends, family, ex-colleagues, the clinic's therapists (they
  know other clinic owners), local business owners you already know.
- Send each a **personal** message — not a broadcast:
  > "Hey ___, I've been building the booking + WhatsApp reminder system the
  > clinic I work with runs on. I'm taking on 3 businesses this month at a
  > reduced rate. Do you know any clinic / studio owner drowning in WhatsApp
  > bookings? Here's a 90-sec video: [link]"
- Goal: 2–4 intro calls from this alone.

### B. Direct outreach (from week 3) — the main engine
**Build the list with your own tool.** Prospector already extracts name,
email, phone and socials from business websites. Target 1–2 cities, 300
businesses in your lane. Score for "has a website but books via WhatsApp/phone".

**The personalised hook ("mini-audit")** — for your best 5 prospects a day,
spend 5 minutes: try to book with them, note the friction, and send:

> **WhatsApp / Instagram DM**
> "Hi Dr. ___ — I tried booking at ___ today; it took 3 messages and I didn't
> get a confirmation or reminder. I build WhatsApp booking + reminder systems
> for clinics — the one I built runs a clinic with 11 therapists. Here's a
> 60-sec demo you can try on your own phone: [demo link]. Worth a 20-min call
> to see what it would look like for you?"

> **Email** — subject: "No-shows at ___?"
> Same message, plus one line of proof and the Loom link. Plain text, no
> attachments, one link.

**Follow-up cadence:** Day 0 → Day 3 (short bump + the video) → Day 7 (a
different angle: "what does one no-show cost you a week?") → Day 14 (close
the loop politely). Most replies come on follow-ups 2–3.

**Volume:** 20 personalised touches a day, 5 days a week = ~100/week.

**Stay safe:**
- WhatsApp: message 1:1 from a separate business number, never bulk-blast
  strangers — unsolicited bulk messages get numbers banned. Stop immediately
  if someone says no.
- Email: send from a separate domain/inbox, max ~30–40 cold emails/day, include
  a way to opt out.
- Only contact publicly listed business contacts, and respect India's DPDP Act
  when storing leads.

### C. Content (from week 2, 3 posts/week) — builds trust for B
- Instagram + LinkedIn. Format: a screen recording + one-line problem.
  "This clinic used to call every client the night before. Now this happens
  automatically →" [reminder arriving on phone].
- Weekly "build in public" post: one automation, what it saves.
- LinkedIn: post the case study, tag no one's private data.
- Content doesn't sell on its own at zero followers — it makes your DMs
  credible when the prospect clicks your profile.

### D. Marketplaces (week 3, low effort, parallel)
- Upwork / Contra / Fiverr: profile for "n8n + WhatsApp automation for
  appointment businesses". Apply to 3–5 jobs/day with the Loom.
- Publish 2–3 sanitised workflows as **n8n templates** (e.g. "WhatsApp
  appointment reminders from Google Calendar") — n8n's template library sends
  inbound traffic to creators.
- Goal: first paid reviews, even small jobs.

### E. Facebook / Instagram ads (only from week 6, and only if B is converting)
Ads amplify an offer that already works — they do not fix one that doesn't.
Switch on only after outreach has produced at least 5 calls and 1 close.
- Objective: **Click-to-WhatsApp** or lead form.
- Audience: 1–2 cities, business owners / page admins, interests in clinic
  and salon management, dentistry, physiotherapy, fitness studios.
- Creative: the 20-second "reminder arrives on the phone" video. Hook:
  "Still calling every patient the night before?"
- Offer in the ad: "Free 20-min no-show audit" or "Try the demo on your phone".
- Budget: ₹500–1,000/day for 14 days (~₹10–15k test). Kill any ad with no
  lead after ₹2,000 spent. Judge on cost per booked call, not clicks.

## 5. The sales call (20-min free audit)

1. **5 min — their day.** "Walk me through what happens from the first
   message to the client leaving." Write down every manual step.
2. **5 min — cost of it.** "How many no-shows a week? What's one session
   worth? Who does the reminders? How long does it take?"
   → Say the number back: "So that's roughly ₹__ a month lost."
3. **5 min — show, don't tell.** Book a slot in the demo on screen; their
   phone gets the WhatsApp.
4. **5 min — next step.** "I'll send a one-page proposal by tomorrow with a
   fixed price and a live date." Send it within 24h. Follow up in 48h.
