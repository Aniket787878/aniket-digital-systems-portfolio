# Frequently Asked Questions

> Demo Physio Clinic is a fictional clinic. The answers below are invented sample text for a portfolio demo.

## Do I need a doctor's referral?
No. You can book directly with us. Some insurers ask for a referral, so please check with your insurer.

## What should I bring to my first visit?
Please bring photo ID, any scan or test reports you have, and a list of your current medicines. Arrive 10 minutes early to fill in the intake form.

## What should I wear?
Comfortable clothing that lets you move freely. Shorts or a loose top are helpful. Towels are provided.

## How long is the first appointment?
The initial assessment takes 60 minutes. Follow-up sessions are 30, 45 or 60 minutes.

## Do you offer video appointments?
Yes. A 30-minute video follow-up is available for existing patients. You need a device with a camera and a stable internet connection.

## Do you bill my insurance directly?
No. We give you an itemised receipt after each session and you claim from your insurer yourself.

## Can I choose my practitioner?
You can ask for a specific practitioner when booking. We will do our best, but it depends on availability.

## Can I bring someone with me?
Yes. A friend or family member is welcome in the room if you wish.
