# 05 — ICP & Positioning

**Status: OPEN QUESTION.** You said "i don't know" on target client. This doc walks the decision.

## Why this matters more than anything else

Your site, offers, outreach copy, and content all get 3-5x sharper the moment you pick an ICP. A vague "I help businesses with automation" converts almost no one. "I build client-intake automations for wellness clinics" converts fast.

**You are not locking yourself in forever.** Pick one for 60 days, learn, adjust.

## Decision framework — pick the ICP that scores highest

Score each candidate 1-5 on:

1. **Domain fluency** — how well do you understand their workflows?
2. **Warm intro path** — can you reach them via someone you know?
3. **Willingness to pay** — do they buy this kind of thing already?
4. **Case study fit** — do your existing projects (booking, consent, team ops) map?
5. **Reachability** — can you find 200 of them on LinkedIn / directories?

## Recommendation

**Start with A (Wellness/clinics, India).** Highest score, warmest intros, your case studies were literally built for this world. Do 30-50 outreach touches + 5-8 posts targeting them, book 3-5 calls, close 1-2, then reassess.

If A stalls after 60 days, D (solo consultants) is a natural expansion.

## Positioning statement to test

> I build automation systems for wellness clinics and studios in India — the kind that quietly handle bookings, client intake, follow-ups and team ops in the background, so your team can stop copy-pasting between WhatsApp, Google Sheets and Instagram.

Test this on your site hero + first 10 cold emails. If reply rate <5%, revise. If >10%, double down.
