## 0. One page summary

- **What:** a premium AI consultancy that makes the front desk of an owner-run service business run itself. Enquiries answered, bookings confirmed, follow-ups sent, reminders and notes done, by AI, checked by a person. The owner owns everything.
- **Who:** owner-run service businesses with 2 to 30 staff. Launch pages: clinics and wellness, studios, coaching and tuition, agencies and consultancies (Aniket's pick, 25 Oct). India first (Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR), then UK, AU, US.
- **Why we win:** the market in India sells ₹2,499 tools and courses. Nobody sells "a person checks every reply", nobody shows the owner proof that every enquiry was followed up, nobody sells the second and third message, nobody says "you own all of it". We sell exactly those four things, with a working system already running in a real practice as proof.
- **How Aniket works (his words, 25 Oct):** advisor first. Map the owner's whole day, then build the full working system fast, train the team and stay, hand over everything. Voice: premium and confident.
- **Offer ladder:** free Enquiry Leak Check > paid Roadmap (₹20k / $490, credited) > Quick Win (₹30k / $690) > Sprint or Assistant (₹40k to ₹1.5L / $1,900 to $4,900) > Front Desk System (quoted) > Care plan (₹15k to ₹30k a month) with a weekly proof note.
- **Name:** Sarvam AI is taken by a famous company. Aniket wants something that stands out; new shortlist in section 4 (Befikr, Pratyek, Sadaiv, Haazir).
- **Where it lives:** inside the portfolio repo (Aniket's pick, 25 Oct): the brand takes over the `/ai` area with its own pages, its own WhatsApp number and Cal.com. Portfolio home links to it.
- **Go-to-market:** founder-led outreach + the free leak check + small Meta and Google tests from month 2. Proof and referrals carry year one, not ads.
- **90 days:** name and domain, pages live, 10 free checks, 3 paid Roadmaps, 1 Care plan.

## 1. The business

### 1.1 Mission
Give small service businesses the front desk a big company has, without hiring one, and without giving up control of their own data and tools.

### 1.2 The problem we own
Money leaks between "someone asked" and "someone answered", and again between "answered" and "booked", and again between "booked" and "came back". Every competitor sells "AI automation" or "never miss a call". We own the whole loop and we prove it weekly.

### 1.3 Who buys and why

| Segment (launch page) | Pain in their words | What we hand to AI first | Why they buy from us |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clinics and wellness | "We miss calls during sessions. Reminders are manual. I reply at 10:40 pm." | Enquiry replies, booking confirmation, reminders, intake forms, session notes | Built and running in a real practice; data handling and consent spelled out |
| Studios (fitness, dance, music) | "Trial class enquiries go cold in a day." | Trial booking, waitlists, renewal nudges, schedule answers | Fast, fixed price, shown live |
| Coaching and tuition | "Parents ask the same 20 questions." | FAQ answers, demo booking, fee reminders | Plain language, WhatsApp-first |
| Agencies and consultancies | "Proposals take days, leads wait." | Lead qualification, proposal drafts, meeting notes to actions | Speaks their language, shows the numbers |

Later pages: real estate, small professional firms, salons.

### 1.4 What we are not
Not an LLM lab. Not a ₹2,499 tool. Not a course. Not a generalist agency with 25 services. Not clinic-only. Not hourly. Not a lock-in.

### 1.5 Proof we have today
- The clinic platform (bookings, consent, notes, WhatsApp, invoicing, recordings, staff roles) in daily use. Anonymised; one hard number to be agreed with the owner.
- Three working demo tools on a dummy clinic (Appointment Desk, Knowledge Assistant, Website Widget) and the Admin Desk demo.
- Self-built tools (consent signer, shared inbox, lead research).
- Missing: testimonials, named case studies. Rule stays: never invent them.

### 1.6 How Aniket really works (his answers, 25 Oct 2026)

| Question | His answer | What the site must say and show |
|---|---|---|
| Day-to-day feel | **Advisor first.** Understanding and planning come first; the build follows the plan | Step 1 on every page is "we map your whole day", not "we install a bot". The Roadmap is the front door |

The four-step method, in client words:
1. **Map.** One working session. We walk your day from the first message to the last invoice and write down where time and money leak.
2. **Build.** The whole flow, not one tool. Live by a date in writing.
3. **Train and stay.** Your team learns it in an afternoon. We fix in days and improve for months.
4. **Hand over.** Your accounts, your data, your playbook. You own every part of it.

### 3.7 Table stakes we must match
1. A free first step with no sign-up (call, quiz, audit). Four competitors have one; we make ours give an instant answer.

5. Hindi and English at least.
6. A visible go-live time (Edesy 7 days, ChatGPT.ca 2 weeks).
7. A clear human handover path and a conversation log.

### 3.8 Gaps we own
1. "A person checks every reply", named and priced (the Care plan). In India nobody sells it.
2. Proof of follow-up: the weekly note. Competitors sell "answering"; nobody sells "you will see it happened".
3. The second and third message. Every ad is about the first reply.
4. One outcome across enquiry, booking and follow-up, not three tools.
5. "You own everything": accounts, data, server, playbook. Nobody says it.
6. Rupee and dollar prices for the same service on one page.
7. Consent and data handling in plain words (DPDP Act). One site in 25 mentions privacy.

### 3.9 The three sales-call comparisons and our one-line answers

3. **Content, two a week.** One real workflow, one owner question answered. Reels and LinkedIn, following `content-principles`.
4. **Partners.** Clinic software resellers, studio management tools, accountants serving the same owners. Referral fee on the Roadmap.
5. **Paid tests from month 2.** Section 5.5 budgets, only after the free check converts organically.
6. **Reviews.** Ask every Roadmap client for a Google review. First five testimonials unlock the proof band.

Skip in year one: cold calling, marketplaces, Fiverr and Upwork, sponsorships, courses.

## 8. Operations

- **Delivery stack:** n8n on own server, Claude models via the Agent Platform or API, WhatsApp Business API through a provider chosen per client in the client's name, Vercel for client pages, the client's existing tools for data.
- **Delivery flow (the four steps):** Map (Roadmap session, written plan in 5 days, fixed quote) > Build (worktree, `build` flow, Playwright user test, fixed date) > Train and stay (team session, 24h fixes, Care plan) > Hand over (accounts, data, playbook in their name).
- **Weekly proof note:** one n8n job per client, Monday 8 am, plain language: enquiries in, answered, booked, followed up, anything a person corrected.
- **Data handling (sell it, do it):** client data stays in the client's accounts; what we hold is listed in the quote; deletion on request; DPDP Act note on the site; health and under-18 data on the stricter path already used for Mindset.
- **Capacity:** one founder. Two builds in flight at most. Roadmaps are the throttle.
- **Costs we carry:** domain, Cal.com, Vercel free, n8n self-hosted. Model and WhatsApp costs pass through.
