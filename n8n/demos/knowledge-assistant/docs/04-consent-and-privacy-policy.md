# Consent and Privacy Policy

> Demo Physio Clinic is a fictional clinic. This policy is invented sample text for a portfolio demo and is not legal advice.

## Consent to treatment
- We ask for written consent before your first treatment. The practitioner explains what will be done and you can ask questions first.
- We ask for verbal consent again at the start of each session before any hands-on technique.
- You can withdraw consent at any time, including in the middle of a session, and you do not need to give a reason.

## Under-16s
Patients under 16 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian, who signs the consent form.

## Chaperones
You may ask for a chaperone at any appointment. Ask the front desk when booking and we will arrange one.

## Your records
- Records are kept for 8 years after your last visit, then securely destroyed.
- Your records are shared only with your consent, or where the law requires it.
- You can ask for a copy of your records at any time (see the Fee List for charges).

## Photos and recordings
We do not take photos or recordings of patients without separate written consent.
