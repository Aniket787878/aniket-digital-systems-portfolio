# Cancellation Policy

> Demo Physio Clinic is a fictional clinic. This policy is invented sample text for a portfolio demo.

## Notice period
Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or move an appointment. You can do this by phone, by email or in the patient portal.

## Late cancellations and no-shows
- Cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice: 50% of the session price is charged.
- No-show (you do not attend and have not told us): 100% of the session price is charged.
- Your first late cancellation is waived once, as a courtesy.

## Exceptions
The clinic manager may waive a fee for genuine emergencies, such as a hospital admission or a family emergency. Please tell the front desk as soon as you can.

## If the clinic cancels
If we have to cancel, we will offer you the next available slot at no charge. If you prefer, we will refund any deposit in full.

## Running late
If you arrive late, the session may be shorter so that the next patient is not delayed. The full session price still applies.
